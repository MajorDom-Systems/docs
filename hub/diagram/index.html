    Module Map¶

    An interactive map of the whole Hub — the Hub itself, the integration SDK, and the Matter / Zigbee / HomeKit integrations — laid out concentrically (Coordinator at the centre, I/O at the rim) and grouped by domain. Click any entity to focus it: everything else mutes, its inputs and outputs light up, and the panel lists what it implements / consumes / is implemented by, the methods callers invoke, its output delegates and its injected dependencies. Search entities, or click a domain's ⤢ label to isolate it.
